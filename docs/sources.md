# Source Registry & Hierarchy

## Rule: Official Sources First
For regulatory and investor-education information, the system must prioritize authoritative sources. Unknown or unsupported claims must be clearly labeled as such. No model is allowed to silently invent official facts.

## Source Hierarchy
1. **Primary Regulatory Bodies:** SEBI (Securities and Exchange Board of India), RBI (Reserve Bank of India).
2. **Official Depositories and Exchanges:** NSDL, CDSL, BSE, NSE.
3. **Government of India Official Portals.**
4. **Official Company Filings:** Annual reports, prospectuses, regulatory disclosures provided by the user.

## Referenced Sources (Phase 0 Research)

### 1. SEBI Investor Survey 2025
- **Relevance:** Confirms the massive demand for investor education on fraud prevention, risk management, and investor rights.
- **Key Insight:** ~47% prefer Hindi, ~47% prefer regional languages, and only ~5% prefer English for investor-education content. Validates our multilingual approach (Hindi, Kannada).

### 2. SANGYAN 2026 Official Site & Unstop Listing
- **Relevance:** Defines Track C (Investor Education for Bharat) requirements, timeline, and judging criteria.
- **Key Constraints:** No stock tips, no trading algorithms, mandatory privacy by design, preference for depth on one real user journey.

### 3. SEBI Due Diligence & Investor Education Material
- **Relevance:** Provides the foundational baseline for what constitutes "educational content" versus "investment advice". Used to guide our safety guardrails.
