# Bharat Investor Copilot - Product Document

## Mission
Build a public-good, multilingual, voice-first financial understanding product for Indian retail investors.

## Problem Statement
Indian investors increasingly have access to financial products and information. However, complex disclosures, financial jargon, English-heavy content, and unfamiliar concepts create a massive gap between having information and actually understanding it. Many investors feel overwhelmed and may act without comprehending the underlying facts or risks.

## Primary Persona
**Kavita (39)**: A Tier-2 homemaker managing family savings. She is not fluent in English and finds financial jargon intimidating. She wants to understand the information she receives (like IPOs, mutual funds, company disclosures) before making any decisions.

## Core User Journey
1. User provides a financial document (PDF, screenshot, or text).
2. System understands the document and extracts key facts.
3. System explains complex jargon in plain language.
4. User asks follow-up questions grounded in the document.
5. User switches to Hindi or Kannada for better comprehension.
6. User interacts via voice (speech-to-text and text-to-speech).
7. System teaches the underlying financial concepts.

## Minimum Viable Product (MVP)
- **Universal Input:** Upload PDF, screenshot/image, or paste text.
- **Plain-Language Reader:** Extract key facts, important terms, and simple summaries from documents.
- **Explain Button:** Break down any extracted fact/term into what it means, why it matters, document context, and an example.
- **Document-Grounded Q&A:** Answer user questions strictly based on the provided document.
- **Multilingual Mode:** Support English, Hindi, and Kannada.
- **Voice Mode:** Voice input and output.
- **Trust Layer:** Clear classification of document-backed facts vs. educational explanations vs. uncertain claims, with visible source references.

## Non-Goals & Explicitly Rejected Features
- NO stock tips or buy/sell/hold recommendations.
- NO personalized investment suitability decisions.
- NO price targets or predictions.
- NO trading signals or automated trading algorithms.
- NO broker promotion or affiliate funnels.
- NO harvesting of SMS, OTPs, contacts, or personal financial records.
